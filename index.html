THE AI PROMPTING PROBLEM

You know what you want.
AI just isn't getting it.

Getting useful results from AI isn't always about using a better AI tool. Sometimes, the difference is simply knowing how to give it better instructions.

01

Generic Answers

You ask AI a question, but the response feels broad, obvious or too generic to actually use.

02

Wrong Format

The information is there, but AI gives it to you in the wrong structure, tone, length or format.

03

Repeating Prompts

You keep rewriting the same instructions because your first prompt didn't produce the result you wanted.

04

Prompt Overload

You give AI too many instructions at once and end up with a complicated prompt and a messy result.

05

Weak Output

Your idea is strong, but the final response still feels unfinished, flat or far from what you imagined.

06

Don't Know How To Fix It

When an AI response misses the mark, you're not sure what to change in your prompt to get a better result.

COPY & PASTE PROMPT

Act as a [ROLE]. Help me [OBJECTIVE]. Here is the context you need: [CONTEXT]. My main constraint is [CONSTRAINT]. Give me the result in [OUTPUT FORMAT].

WHY IT WORKS

It gives the AI enough context to understand the task, the desired result and the boundaries it needs to work within instead of leaving important details to guesswork.
